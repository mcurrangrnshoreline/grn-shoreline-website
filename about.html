About Us

We advise wealth management professionals and firms with clarity and integrity. Our deep industry network, proven processes & commitment to education ensures top support.

We don't pitch – we listen, strategize, and align opportunities with your unique and personal vision. Our strength lies in helping advisors and firms find the right-fit culture & business model.

Whether you're joining a new firm, or building your own, we provide strategic insights to make sound, confident decisions. We empower professionals by sharing best practices, market trends, and decades of industry wisdom.

From the Desk of Matthew Curran

GRN Shoreline was founded to Guide Wealth Management Advisors to a Perfect Fit. Our team combines exceptional service and responsiveness with a knowledge of - and a deep network across - this complex industry.

With that background, our mission is to:

  • ✓ Help advisors and teams think ahead and anticipate steps ahead.
  • ✓ Assist wealth management advisors and teams navigate career inflection points with clarity.
  • ✓ Advise when the current structure still works today but does not align with your future vision.

Through The Advisor Transition Architecture™, we replace reactive transitions with deliberate sequencing, personal readiness, and a new promise worth launching.

We've seen what happens when advisors wait too long—and what becomes possible when they slow down, regain leverage, and move on their own terms.

We don't sell destinations. We help you design what comes next—so the transition strengthens your identity, your business, and your optionality. We start with readiness. With identity. With sequencing decisions in a way that preserves control.

— Matthew Curran, Founder & Managing Partner
