Case Study

Reclaiming Flexibility and Client Impact After 27 Years at a Wirehouse

Steve Cooper built his $1B book the old-school way: relentless networking, deep relationships, and consistent delivery over 29 years. But over time, the firm that once enabled his growth started working against him.

He was forced to turn away 4 complex lending deals totaling $310mm in credit business - his firm simply wouldn't compete. His frustration wasn't about ego. It was about client experience and service. If he couldn't deliver, someone else would.

When we first connected, Steve wasn't seeking a move. He was seeking options. What else was possible that wouldn't jeopardize his team, his clients, or his control?
